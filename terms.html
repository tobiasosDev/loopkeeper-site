Free and Pro

Loopkeeper is free for up to 3 projects in progress at a time, with every core feature, including backups. Loopkeeper Pro adds unlimited projects and turning photos and scanned PDFs into rows.

Yearly subscription

  • $19.99 per year in the US (local prices vary), after a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID when the trial ends, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before.
  • The subscription renews automatically each year unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Manage or cancel any time in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Lifetime

A one-time purchase of $39.99 in the US (local prices vary) that unlocks Pro with no subscription.

Restore earlier purchases on a new device with Restore purchases in the app. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your patterns

You decide which patterns you add to Loopkeeper. Please only add patterns you have the right to use, and respect the designers who wrote them. Loopkeeper keeps your patterns on your device and never shares or publishes them.

Accuracy

Loopkeeper splits your pattern into rows and reads stitch counts automatically, and it reads scans with text recognition. Both can make mistakes. Check the rows against your original pattern; the original is always the reference.
