Common questions

Where do I get patterns?

Loopkeeper works with the patterns you bring: PDFs from Etsy or Ravelry, free patterns from designers and yarn companies, or pages from your books. In the app, open New project, then Where to find patterns.

How do I move my projects to a new iPhone?

On the old iPhone, open Settings in Loopkeeper and tap Create backup file, then save it to Files or send it to yourself. On the new iPhone, tap Restore from backup. Your device backup through iCloud also includes Loopkeeper.

I bought Pro. How do I get it back?

Open Settings in Loopkeeper and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Loopkeeper Pro. If you cancel during the free trial, you are not charged. Loopkeeper reminds you two days before the trial ends.

A scan has mistakes

Scan in good light with the whole page flat and in view. You can fix any row with Project and pattern › Edit pattern text.
