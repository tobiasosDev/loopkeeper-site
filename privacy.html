Privacy policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Loopkeeper does not collect personal data. There is no account, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party analytics in the app. Your projects and patterns stay on your iPhone.

Who is responsible

TecMinds GmbH, Neustadtstrasse 8a, 6003 Luzern, Switzerland. Email: info@tecminds.ch.

What stays on your device

Project names, yarn and hook notes, pattern text, imported PDFs, photos you add, and your row and stitch counts are stored only inside the app on your device. We have no server and never receive them. They are included in your device backup if you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer; that backup is handled by Apple under your settings.

A backup file is created only when you tap Create backup file in Settings. Where it goes is up to you.

Camera and photos

When you scan a paper pattern or pick a photo, the image is read on your device with Apple's built-in text recognition. Images and text are not uploaded.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your name, Apple ID or payment details. Apple gives us anonymized sales reports.

App Store statistics

Apple provides developers with aggregated statistics, such as downloads and crash reports, only from people who chose to share analytics with developers in their device settings. These statistics do not identify you.

Notifications

If you start a free trial, Loopkeeper can schedule a local reminder two days before the trial ends. It is created on your device; no data is sent to us.

Links to other websites

The app links to pattern websites such as Ravelry or Etsy. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you and have it corrected or deleted. Because the app collects none, we normally hold none. Write to info@tecminds.ch.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. If the app ever starts collecting data, we will say so here before that version is released.
